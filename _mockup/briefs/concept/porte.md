# File: D/concept-porte.html — «Due mondi», objects floating on velvet
Replaces the two door cards «Pietre e rituali» / «Cura di sé» (section `.doors` in home-calma.html, right under the hero). Context above: bottom of the hero; below: top of «I più scelti».

## Idea
Two side-by-side halves on the dark page (stacked on <900px). Each half: h3-level title in Baskervville large (≈ clamp(30px,3vw,44px)) — «Pietre e *rituali*» and «Cura di *sé*» — a one-line intro (reuse the door intros from home-calma.html), then a still-life STAGE where the 6 real product cutouts of that world (BZ_CONCEPT.porte.pietre / .cura, one per subcategory) float on the dark like objects on velvet. The section has one h2 (visually hidden ok: «Le collezioni») or the two titles as h2s, your call, but semantic.
- Stage: about 560px tall at 1440 (each half ~ 640px wide), a very subtle velvet: radial-gradient of #201e1b → transparent, plus assets/grain-160.png at 4% opacity. A hairline gold 1px divider (gold 25%) between the two halves on desktop.
- Composition: asymmetric still life, NOT a grid. Hand-place the 6 objects with absolute % positions and different scales (one hero object ~ 220-260px tall, two medium ~150-180, three small ~100-130), some overlap in depth (z-index by depth), baselines at different heights like objects on a shelf seen from above at an angle. Keep 24px+ breathing room between objects. Use each cutout's natural aspect ratio (img natural sizes: see the files; pendolo is tall and thin, mala and gioielli are wide). Give each a `data-depth` 1-3.
- Each object = `<a class="obj" href="<collection url>">` containing the `<img src="img/cutout/<key>.webp" alt="<product title>">` and the contact shadow element. Under each object a soft glow is NOT needed; only the contact shadow.
- Desktop pointer parallax: on pointermove over the stage, translate each object by (2 + depth*2) px max in the pointer direction, rAF-throttled, transform only, off on touch/hover:none and reduced motion. Stage resets smoothly on pointerleave.
- Hover/focus on an object: lift (translateY(-6px) scale(1.04), 240ms), its shadow shrinks/fades a bit, and a small label appears next to it (on the side with more room): subcategory name in gold small caps 11px letter-spacing .14em (ensure contrast ≥4.5:1, use #d4b98a if needed), product title in Figtree 14px ivory (max 2 lines), price in Figtree 600, and a '+' button (adds that product). Label: dark card #201e1b at 92%, 1px line rgba(201,169,110,.3), radius 14px, padding 12px 14px, opacity/transform transition 200ms. The label must be reachable by keyboard: focusing the link shows it, Tab moves to its '+'. Clicking the object (not the '+') goes to the subcategory collection.
- Under each stage: a quiet line of the 6 subcategory text links separated by « · » (Figtree 14px #cdc4b4, hover ivory), then a text-link «Vedi tutto →» to pietre-e-rituali.html / cura-di-se.html.
- Entrance: on first view (IntersectionObserver, once) the objects rise in: from translateY(18px) opacity 0, 400ms each, staggered 60ms. Not with ?static=1 or reduced motion.
- Mobile (<900px): each world becomes: title + intro, then a horizontal scroll-snap row (overflow-x:auto, scroll-snap-type x mandatory, padding-inline 20px, peek of the next item) of the 6 objects, each in a 64vw max 260px slot ~ 300px tall with the cutout centred on its contact shadow, and the label ALWAYS visible under it (subcategory, title, price, '+'). No parallax. Then the text links line.
- `?state=hover` forces the hover label on the hero object of «Pietre e rituali» (for the screenshot).
