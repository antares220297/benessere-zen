# File: D/concept-intenzione.html — «La ruota delle intenzioni»
Replaces «Scegli per intenzione» in home-calma.html. Context above: bottom of «Scegli la tua pietra»; below: top of «Un pezzo da regalare». Data: BZ_CONCEPT.intenzioni (5 entries, each: key, name, stone, color, copy, cutout, collection, items[3] with role Pietra/Da portare/Rituale, total).

## Idea
Section title «Scegli per *intenzione*» + one line «Gira la ruota: per ogni intenzione, una pietra e il suo piccolo set.» Desktop (≥1024): two columns, the dial left (~520px square), the set right (rest of the width, vertically centred, max ~560px), both on the plain dark page (no card around the dial).
- Dial: concentric thin rings (SVG circles or CSS borders, 1px, gold at 18-40% alpha, 3-4 rings at different radii, one dashed very faint with stroke-dasharray 1 6), plus 60 tiny tick marks on one ring made with SVG `<line>`s or a repeating-conic-gradient mask (plain geometry). On the OUTER ring, the 5 intention names are set as text on a circular path (SVG `<textPath>` on a circle path), evenly spaced at 72°, Baskervville italic ~22px, ivory at 70%; the selected one turns gold #c9a96e full opacity. Like the approved dosha orbit labels, but this is a rotating dial, not spheres.
- The names themselves are the tabs: implement a `role="tablist"` (aria-label «Intenzioni») with 5 `role="tab"` buttons. The SVG text is decorative (aria-hidden); overlay transparent hit areas or use real buttons positioned on the ring so clicking a name selects it. Arrow keys / Home / End move selection (roving tabindex), aria-selected, aria-controls → the set panel (`role="tabpanel"`).
- Selecting rotates the whole names ring (a `<g>` with transform rotate) so the selected name comes to the TOP centre (12 o'clock), 400ms ease cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1), taking the shortest direction. A small fixed gold marker at 12 o'clock (a 1px gold line 14px long or a 5px dot) shows "selected here".
- Centre: the real stone cutout (item.cutout, a cluster of tumbled stones, ~ 46% of dial width) on a contact shadow, with a soft glow in the stone's color behind it (radial-gradient of `color` at ~35% alpha → transparent, large and blurred, muted not neon). On change, crossfade stone + glow (opacity 300ms, slight scale .96→1). Preload all 5 cutouts.
- Set panel (right): small caps label «Il set per la <name lower>» (gold, contrast ok), stone name in Baskervville 40px («Ametista»), the one-line copy (Figtree 16px #cdc4b4), then 3 compact rows: 64px photo, role in small caps (Pietra / Da portare / Rituale), title (link to the product url), price, '+' button. Rows separated by 1px lines rgba(238,231,219,.1). Then a primary gold button «Aggiungi il set · 35,80 €» (total from data, formatted) — this is THE gold element of the screen; and a text-link «Vedi tutti i pezzi →» to item.collection. Small note line below: «La foto mostra il tipo di pietra: ogni pezzo è un po' diverso.» (12-13px #cdc4b4). Panel content crossfades (opacity + translateY 8px, 250ms) on change. Free shipping hint optional: none.
- Auto-select Serenità at load (no animation at load). `?state=coraggio` (or any key) selects that intention at load.
- Mobile/tablet (<1024): the dial becomes a HALF dial at the top: show only the top half of the rings (clip the SVG, ~340px wide at 390, up to 520 at 768), the names on the arc, the selected at top; the stone sits inside the half circle on the baseline; below, the set panel full width. Also provide 2 small prev/next buttons (‹ ›, aria-labels «Intenzione precedente / successiva») at the ends of the half dial baseline, and swipe left/right on the dial (pointer events) changes intention. Ensure the 5 names on the half arc are all readable or that the non-visible ones are reachable via prev/next and the tablist (the tabs can also be listed as a visually small row of names under the half dial if the arc can't show all 5; your call, keep it elegant).
- Must NOT look like the dosha spheres (no overlapping circles/Venn) nor the stone flip cards: it is dial + set.
- Reduced motion: no rotation animation (instant), fades only.
- Disclaimer once at the bottom of the section: «I significati appartengono alla tradizione e non sostituiscono il parere di un medico.» 12px #cdc4b4.
