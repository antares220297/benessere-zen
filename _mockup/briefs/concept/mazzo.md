# File: D/concept-porte-mazzo.html — «Il mazzo»
Alternative for the two door cards «Pietre e rituali» / «Cura di sé» (section `.doors` in home-calma.html, under the hero). Context above: bottom of the hero; below: top of «I più scelti». Data: BZ_CONCEPT.porte.pietre and .cura (6 each: key, sub (subcategory name), collection, cutout, title, price, fromPrice).

## Idea
The 12 subcategories become tall tarot-proportioned cards (ratio 2:3.5) fanned in an arc like a hand of cards, two fans side by side on desktop («Pietre e *rituali*» left, «Cura di *sé*» right), each fan under its Baskervville title (clamp(30px,3vw,44px)) and a one-line intro (reuse the door intros of home-calma.html).
- Card (≈ 170×298px at 1440): background #1c1a17 with a subtle radial glow (rgba(201,169,110,.10) at 50% 45%), radius 14px, 1px border rgba(201,169,110,.28) and a thin gold INNER frame inset 8px (1px rgba(201,169,110,.45), radius 9px; a second hairline inset 11px at 20% is welcome). Top: a small Roman numeral (I–VI) in Baskervville 13px gold, centred, letter-spacing .2em. Middle: the real product cutout (img/cutout/<key>.webp), contained in ~70% width, with a contact shadow under it and a soft glow behind (radial, muted). Bottom: subcategory name in Baskervville italic 17px ivory, centred, like a tarot title, max 2 lines; above it a tiny hairline.
- Fan: 6 cards overlapping, each rotated around a common origin far below (transform-origin 50% 220% or similar), angles from about -21° to +21° (step ~8.4°), so the tops form an arc. Container height enough to show the arc fully with no clipping, and the two fans together use the full width at 1440 (each fan ~600px wide).
- Hovering the fan (or focus within) spreads it slightly (angles ×1.25, transform only, 300ms). Hovering/focusing a card lifts it (translateY(-18px) along its own axis, z-index top) and reveals «da X €» (fromPrice formatted, Figtree 13px, ivory on a small dark pill at the bottom of the card, above the title or replacing the hairline), 200ms. Each card is an `<a href="collection">` with an accessible name «<sub>, da X €». Clicking opens the collection.
- Under each fan: a quiet line of the 6 subcategory text links (« · »), and «Vedi tutto →».
- Entrance (once, not with ?static=1 / reduced motion): the cards deal out from the closed stack into the fan, 400ms, staggered 60ms.
- Mobile (<900px): each world becomes a horizontal swipe deck: overflow-x:auto, scroll-snap-type x mandatory, cards ~ 62vw max 230px wide upright (no rotation), gap 14px, peek of the next card, «da X €» always visible. Tablet 768-1023: fans stacked one per row, centred.
- `?state=hover` forces: left fan spread + card III («Pietre grezze») lifted with its price (for the screenshot).
- No custom illustrations, no icons: only CSS frames, numerals, cutouts.
