# File: D/concept-intenzione-carte.html — «Pesca la tua intenzione»
Alternative for «Scegli per intenzione» in home-calma.html. Context above: bottom of «Scegli la tua pietra»; below: top of «Un pezzo da regalare». Data: BZ_CONCEPT.intenzioni (5: key, name, stone, color, copy, cutout, collection, items[3] with role, total).

## Idea
Title «Scegli per *intenzione*». 5 face-down cards in a row (desktop: centred row, each ≈ 200×340px, ratio ~ 2:3.4, gap 22px, the row uses the width generously). Under the row: «Chiudi gli occhi, pensa a cosa ti serve, scegli una carta.» (Baskervville italic 20px, #cdc4b4).
- Card BACK (all identical): dark #1c1a17, radius 14px, 1px border rgba(201,169,110,.3), inner hairline frame inset 9px; a pattern made ONLY with CSS (e.g. repeating-radial-gradient fine gold rings at 10-14% alpha centred, plus a conic/linear fine line pattern very faint), and in the centre the wordmark text «Benessere *Zen*» in Baskervville (gold italic «Zen») 15px, plus a tiny «·» row. No drawn symbols, no icons.
- Card FRONT: dark card with a soft radial glow in the intention's `color` (alpha ≤ .35), the stone cutout (item.cutout) centred with a contact shadow, the intention name in Baskervville 26px ivory at the bottom and the stone name in small caps gold 11px (contrast ok) above it, the one-line copy NOT on the card (it goes in the panel).
- Each card is a fixed intention (order: Serenità, Coraggio, Protezione, Affetti, Concentrazione), no randomness. Each card is a `<button aria-expanded aria-controls="set-panel">` with aria-label «Carta 1 di 5» before reveal and «Serenità, rivelata» after.
- Click/Enter/Space: the card rises (translateY(-14px)) and turns over with ONE 3D rotateY flip, 400ms (perspective 1200px, backface-visibility hidden, faces `inert`-safe). The other 4 cards dim to opacity .35 and scale .97 (still clickable: clicking another one puts the current back face-down and reveals the new one). Then the SET panel appears (opacity + translateY 10px, 300ms, after the flip) BELOW the row on desktop, full container width, in two columns: left the stone name in Baskervville 38px + copy (Figtree 16 #cdc4b4) + note «La foto mostra il tipo di pietra: ogni pezzo è un po' diverso.» 13px; right 3 compact product rows (64px photo, role in small caps Pietra / Da portare / Rituale, title link, price, '+' button), then the gold primary button «Aggiungi il set · 35,80 €» (formatted total; the only gold-filled element) and the text-link «Vedi tutti i pezzi →» (item.collection). Plus a small text button «Pesca di nuovo» (resets all cards face-down, panel hidden, focus back to the first card).
- Before any draw the panel is hidden (hidden attribute), the section is just the row + the line: keep it compact, no empty space.
- Keyboard: Tab through cards, Enter/Space reveals, focus moves to the panel heading after reveal (tabindex -1), «Pesca di nuovo» works.
- Reduced motion: no 3D flip, crossfade back→front 200ms; no rise.
- Mobile (<900px): the 5 cards in a horizontal scroll-snap row (cards ~ 46vw max 190px, peek), the line under it, and the panel under the line full width, rows stacked; the set button full width ≥48px.
- `?state=coraggio` (any key) reveals that card at load without animation (for the screenshot); default = all face-down.
- Disclaimer once at the bottom: «I significati appartengono alla tradizione e non sostituiscono il parere di un medico.» 12px #cdc4b4.
